Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Clip Studio Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information we access

When you authorize the App with TikTok, it receives an access token and basic profile information (such as your display name and open ID) through the TikTok Login Kit, and uses the Content Posting API to upload videos you choose.

How information is used

This information is used only to upload and publish videos to your TikTok account and to confirm the upload succeeded. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. The App does not operate a server or database that stores user data.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.

Contact

Questions can be submitted via GitHub.
